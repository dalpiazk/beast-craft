# Beast Craft balance simulator report

Generated by `Tooling/BalanceSim` (local-only tooling, not a CI job). These numbers are inputs to design
decisions; nothing here is applied to the roster automatically. Battles are random (damage variance and crits),
but every battle's rng is seeded from the inputs, so the run is deterministic: the same roster, fixtures, code
and arguments produce a byte-identical report. Battles per PvE team and fight, and per PvP game: 5 (`--samples`), each with its own seed.

- Roster: `BeastCraft/Assets/_Project/Data/Creatures/beast-roster.json` (10 species)
- Sections: PvE team vs encounter (primary); PvP 1v1 round-robin (secondary)

## Standard beast kit

No skills are authored yet, so every beast fights with the same kit and its stat line is what gets measured.
Fire priority is the order below; every skill aims at the nearest enemy.

| Skill | Category | Shape | Range | Power | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Blast | Special | SingleTarget | 3 | 40 | 1 |
| Strike | Physical | SingleTarget | 1 | 55 | 1 |
| Burst (physical half) | Physical | AreaBurst (radius 2 around the caster) | 2 | 20 | 2 |
| Burst (special half) | Special | AreaBurst (radius 2 around the caster) | 2 | 20 | 2 |

Strike and Blast share a cooldown; Strike's higher power offsets its range-1 handicap (it fires about 0.73x as
often as Blast), and Burst is split into equal physical and special halves, so `Attack` and `SpecialAttack` (and
`Defense` / `SpecialDefense` on the receiving end) carry about equal weight; the PvE kit parity table checks it.
Burst goes last so it fires from the tile the beast has just walked to. `elemental` mode: every kit skill carries
the beast's first element; `neutral`: all are `None`, isolating the stat lines from the element chart.

## PvE: team vs encounter (primary)

The expected shape of the game: the player fields a team of beasts against enemies that are not roster beasts,
from one huge creature to a couple of dozen small ones. Every team is fielded against every encounter, each
encounter's difficulty is calibrated so the average team clears it about 50.0% of the time (where a beast's
presence moves the outcome most), and each beast is judged by how much it moves its team's clear rate.

### PvE configuration

- Teams: every combination of 4 distinct beasts (210 teams, format SmallGroup); each beast is in 84 of them
- Samples: damage variance (90-110%) and crits make a battle random, so every team fights every calibration step 5 times with distinct seeds
  (1050 battles per evaluation; a beast's per-cell metrics rest on 420 battles with it and 630 without). Seeds exclude the multiplier, so
  calibration compares multipliers on the same rolls; the clear rate is over every sample.
- Levels: 1, 50, 100 (beasts and enemies at the same level); kit modes: `elemental`, `neutral`; no gear; no avatar
- Encounters: `Tooling/BalanceSim/encounters.json` — simulator fixtures, not game content; enemy elements: as authored (enemy kits are `None` in `neutral` mode, so an elementless encounter reads the same in both modes)
- Placement: each side takes the front-most tiles of its own deployment zone (front row first, then outward from
  the centre line); enemies in fixture order, the team through `PlacementValidator.TryPlaceAll`. Which team member
  gets which slot (and unit id, the initiative-tie and target-tie break within the team) is a fixed seeded shuffle per team.
- Initiative ties between the sides: `TurnManager` breaks equally full, equally fast gauges on the ordinal unit id,
  so each battle prefixes one side's ids so that it wins cross-side ties; in every (kit mode, encounter, level) cell
  exactly half the teams win them (a seeded shuffle of the team indices). The prefix is side-wide, so ties within a
  side are unchanged.
- Difficulty: HP, Atk, Def, SpA and SpD of every enemy are scaled by one multiplier per (kit mode, encounter,
  level); Speed and Move are not. Calibration starts at x1, doubles or halves until the 50.0% target is bracketed
  (x0.016 to x64.000), then bisects 8 times; the evaluated multiplier whose clear rate is closest to the
  target wins (first evaluated on a tie). Every metric below is measured at that multiplier.
- Movement rules are the Runtime's own (`BattleTurnExecutor`), with no simulator-side emulation: a defeated unit
  leaves the grid the moment it falls, and a unit that cannot reach range this turn makes a partial approach
  (walks its remaining move toward the target and holds the skill).
- Combat stances are the Runtime's too (`CombatStance`, from the roster and the fixtures): a Vanguard approaches
  as above and prefers stop tiles that screen its Ranged / Skirmisher allies; a Ranged unit never walks into melee
  (Strike fires only on an adjacent target); Ranged and Skirmisher units prefer stop tiles with fewer adjacent
  enemies and spend leftover movement backing away, keeping the nearest enemy within their longest reach.
- Turn order: the Runtime's ATB gauge (`TurnManager`): every unit fills a gauge by its Speed and acts at 1000, so twice the
  Speed is twice the turns. Battle time is normalized: 1.0 = one turn of a Speed-100 unit. Speed scales with level, so the
  same fight reads longer at low levels; compare times within a level, not across levels.
- Max time: 2000 (a battle reaching it is a stalemate and counts as not cleared); base seed: 12345
- Metrics: **marginal** = clear rate of teams containing the beast minus teams without it (points; the primary
  number). **Dmg share** / **Taken share** = the beast's share of its team's damage dealt / taken (HP actually
  removed, so overkill is not counted), averaged over its teams. **Survival** = standing at the end. **Time to
  clear** = mean length of the clears it took part in (normalized time). **Turns / time** = the beast's turns per
  unit of time over its battles (Speed / 100 while standing). "Overall" averages every encounter and level equally.

### Kit parity (beast skill fires at calibrated difficulty, all levels)

Strike (Physical, range 1) fires less often than Blast (Special, range 3): it needs the beast to reach a free
tile next to its target, and a Ranged beast only fires it at an enemy already adjacent. Strike's higher power
compensates; **physical share** = Strike fires x Strike power as a share of all single-target power delivered,
and 50% means `Attack` and `SpecialAttack` weigh the same. Burst uses count once per pair of halves; targets =
enemies inside the radius when the first half fires.

## PvP: 1v1 round-robin (secondary)

Kept for reference: the game is expected to be PvE, so a beast's 1v1 record against the other roster beasts is a
secondary signal. Same standard kit as the PvE section.

### PvP configuration

- Levels: 1, 50, 100; kit modes: `elemental`, `neutral`; no gear; no avatar
- Arena: Medium, 1v1, mirrored central start tiles in each deployment zone
- Round-robin: every pair of distinct species, played twice per level and mode with sides swapped, each game 5 times with distinct seeds (damage variance and crits make battles random) (90 games per beast per level per mode; mirror matches skipped); 2700 battles total
- Win rate = wins / games; mutual defeats and stalemates count as games but not wins. Flags: win rate above 60.0% or below 40.0%, a swing of more than 25.0 points across levels, and any stalemate.
- Turn order: the Runtime's ATB gauge (`TurnManager`: each unit acts every 1000 / Speed ticks). Battle length is normalized time, 1.0 = one turn of a Speed-100 unit, so it reads longer at low levels, where Speed is lower; max time 2000.
