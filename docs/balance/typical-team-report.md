# Beast Craft typical-team report

Generated by `dotnet run --project Tooling/BalanceSim -c Release -- --mode typical --gear typical --write-difficulty content/data/Encounters/encounter-difficulty.json --out docs/balance/typical-team-report.md`
(see Tooling/BalanceSim/README.md, "Typical team"). The shipping difficulty (`encounter-difficulty.json`) and the region bosses'
`DifficultyOverride`s, calibrated so a TYPICAL team (a sensible, not optimal pick from the beasts the player owns there) clears
each kind of fight's target; beside every cell, the spread a weaker and a stronger pick see. This is not the per-beast
balance guard, which stays on the full-roster scouted pick at 50% (`--mode pve --seeds ... --target-clear 50`).

## Configuration

- Owned roster (the Kinship flow): every one-per-stance trio (30, the Hearthglen trio), plus one beast of every Kinship site whose trial level is at most the band's level (trio k
  takes offer (k + site) mod 2 of the site's two: both choices, half the trios each). The trio at the band's level (the pacing
  model's fielded median), each recruit the campaign model's bench gap below it (3 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 5 / 5 levels in r01 / r02 / ..., the last after).
- Reasonable teams: three owned beasts covering at least two stances, at least one a Vanguard (no screen for the Ranged
  without one; a single-stance team gives up the stance system).
- **Typical** (calibrated): per roster the median reasonable team's clear rate (each team over every composition, at its
  members' levels, default placement, no scouting), the mean over the rosters (lowest-highest roster). **Weak**: the roster's
  lower-quartile team. **Best**: the roster's best team (the luckiest of its teams: an upper bound). **Scouted**: the game's
  `TeamSuggester` over what the roster owns, per composition. **Strong**: the scouted pick with upgraded skills (the focus
  skill's level on every skill). Placement is the simulator's default in every column (a better placement is not modelled).
- Skills: every beast and avatar skill at the pacing model's typical team level (`--mode pacing`, "Team skill level");
  the strong pick at the upgraded level. Gear: `--gear typical` at the band's level. The library avatar at the band's level.
- Encounters: 8 generated compositions per shape (seed 12345), 2 battle(s) per team and composition at every step; `--obstacles r01` for the generated shapes (the table is region-agnostic), each boss on its own region's battlefields.
- Search: from x1, doubling or halving to bracket the target, then 8 bisections; the closest evaluated multiplier wins. Elemental cells only (the game reads them).
- Before = the table (or override) this run read; After = this run's.
